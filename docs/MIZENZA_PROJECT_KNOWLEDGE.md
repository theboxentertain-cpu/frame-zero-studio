## Domain principles
(a) Store separately writer-established FACTS, AI/machine PROPOSALS, director APPROVED DECISIONS.
(b) Film => sequences => scenes => shots => media assets, each with stable IDs, owner, version and upstream dependencies.
(c) Treatment: film-wide visual/acting/sound philosophy; sequence arc & reversals; scene objective/obstacle/tactic/subtext/blocking/visual stakes. Do not hallucinate scenes from loglines.
(d) Shot Lab: every shot answers dramatic change, subject, position, internal action, why cut, continuity. Track lens rationale, framing, blocking, duration, movement trigger and end. For moving shots, no approval without motivated movement details.
(e) Living Actor: objective, obstacle, tactics, partner's cue, listening, reaction, physical behavior, moment-to-moment change, beat-level tactics. No simplistic 'sad 80%' sliders. Current system stores only WRITTEN actor plans until performance generation exists.
(f) Casting: diverse individual facial attributes; regional culture kept separate from appearance and identity. Do not stereotype regions/ethnicity. No actor likeness/voice cloning without appropriate rights/consent.
(g) World Bibles: country/governorate/city/district/year, architecture, interiors, clothing, props, transport, environment/sounds, evidence provenance and local expert review status. A user's lock is not historical/cultural validation.
(h) Dialect & Voice Director: Egyptian Arabic as quality flagship; allow region-specific pronunciation, register, code-switching, dialogue, pauses, overlaps, speaking objective, consent metadata. Written plan NOT synthesized voice. No accent assigned by appearance/region automatically.
(i) References: approved abstract cinematic techniques, not infringing replications of movies/directors/music.
(j) Six production approval gates: treatment, world, shot_list, film_stills, motion, final_cut. Never pre-approve empty/placeholder output. Invalidate downstream approvals on changed source while preserving human edits for reconciliation.
(k) Media generations planned: individual video clips typically short; up-to-three-minute edited sequence assembled from multiple shots; aspirational feature-length via multiple sequences; do NOT generate one 60-minute text-to-video clip.
(l) Technical: robust service isolation so model providers are swappable; transparent generation budgets/credits; human review.
